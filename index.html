Living away from your parents can sometimes be difficult. You may worry about their daily needs, doctor appointments, shopping, mobile services or simply whether they have someone to talk to.

SUKHOBAS is here to provide friendly local assistance and companionship to elderly people in Kolkata. We believe that a little help, a little time and genuine care can make everyday life more comfortable and enjoyable.

When you live away, even simple things can become difficult for your parents.ছোট ছোট প্রয়োজনগুলোই কখনও কখনও বড় সমস্যা হয়ে দাঁড়ায়।

You call your parents every day. You ask whether they are fine. They always say everything is okay. But sometimes:

  • Mother needs help ordering groceries online.
  • Father is struggling with mobile recharge.
  • They need someone to accompany them to a doctor.
  • Medical tests need to be arranged.
  • Medicines or household essentials need to be collected.
  • They want to visit a temple but do not have company.
  • Sometimes they simply want someone to sit and talk with them.

These may seem like small things, but they matter. SUKHOBAS wants to make these everyday moments easier.

A Little Care. A Little Time. A Lot of Trust.একটু যত্ন, একটু সময়, অনেকটা ভরসা।

SUKHOBAS (সুখবাস) is a Kolkata-based initiative dedicated to supporting senior citizens with everyday assistance, companionship and practical help.

Our aim is to make life easier and more enjoyable for elderly people while giving their families greater confidence when they cannot be physically present.

We understand that elderly parents need more than just someone to complete a task. They need patience, understanding, respect and a friendly presence. That is the spirit behind SUKHOBAS.

Getting Support Is Simple.সাহায্য পাওয়া সহজ।

  1. Contact Us

    Call or WhatsApp us and explain what kind of support your parents need.

  2. Understand Your Requirements

    We discuss the location, requirements, preferences and suitable arrangements with the family.

  3. Plan the Assistance

    We discuss the service, schedule, availability and applicable charges before confirming.

  4. Provide Support

    Our team coordinates the agreed assistance for your parents.

  5. Stay Connected

    We communicate with the family about the assistance provided and any further requirements.

Simple communication. Thoughtful assistance. A little more peace of mind.

Frequently Asked Questionsসাধারণ প্রশ্নোত্তর

What is SUKHOBAS?

SUKHOBAS is a Kolkata-based initiative that supports elderly people through everyday assistance, companionship and practical coordination.

Who can use SUKHOBAS services?

Senior citizens living in Kolkata and families who need local assistance for their elderly parents can contact us.

Can children living abroad contact SUKHOBAS?

Yes. Family members living in other cities or countries can contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email.

What kind of everyday assistance do you provide?

We help with grocery requirements, essential items, mobile recharge, smartphone assistance, online orders and other agreed everyday needs.

Can you accompany my parents to the doctor?

We can discuss and coordinate doctor appointment assistance and accompaniment based on the location, availability and agreed arrangements.
