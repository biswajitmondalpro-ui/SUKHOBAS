Special Experiences

At SUKHOBAS, we believe that elderly people should continue enjoying the activities they love.

A visit to a temple, a Durga Puja pandal, a favourite local place or simply an afternoon outing can create wonderful memories. We want to encourage meaningful experiences and opportunities for senior citizens to remain socially connected.
